Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No account. No server. No analytics. Your calendar stays inside macOS, prayer times are computed on device, and the only thing Crest ever fetches is its own update. The code is open, so you don't have to take our word for it.

Questions, answered

Before you ask.

Is it really free?

Really. Crest is open source and built as sadaqah jariyah, an ongoing act of charity. No trial, no premium tier, no catch. If it helps you, share it with someone.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Yes. Crest reads whatever calendars macOS knows about, including Google, iCloud, and Outlook accounts you've added to the system. No OAuth screens, no separate sign-in.

I'm not Muslim. Is Crest still for me?

Absolutely. The prayer layer is optional and off by default. Out of the box, Crest is a fast menu bar calendar with unmissable meeting alerts.

Won't a full screen takeover annoy me?

That's the point, but only for the moments you choose. Everything is opt-in per prayer and per alert type, snooze is always one keystroke away, and Crest can respect Do Not Disturb.

Why does macOS warn me on first launch?

Crest isn't notarized with Apple yet, so macOS is being careful. Right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again. It happens exactly once, and the code is public if you'd like to check what you're running.
