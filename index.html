Prayer times

Work gets loud. Prayer stays quiet.

Between calls and deadlines, Asr can slip away without a sound. Crest gives every prayer a voice.

When the waqt begins, a calm teal reminder fills the screen. When the window is closing, a firmer amber warning steps in before it's too late. And jamaat gets its own golden reminder, timed to your mosque.

Times are calculated on your Mac with the Adhan library, with your choice of calculation method, madhab, and per-prayer adjustments. Even the Hijri date respects your local moon sighting.

Islamic Mode is optional and off by default.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No account. No server. No analytics. Your calendar stays inside macOS, prayer times are computed on device, and the only thing Crest ever fetches is its own update.

Crest is free, open source, and built as sadaqah jariyah, an ongoing act of charity. If it helps you keep your rhythm, pass it along to someone who needs theirs back.

Contributions, translations, and du'as are all welcome.
